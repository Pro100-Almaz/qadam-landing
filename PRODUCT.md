# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Parents in Kazakhstan choosing a primary school (grades 1–7) for their child, mostly browsing on phones, in Russian or Kazakh. They compare schools, look for trust signals (team, program, results) and want an easy way to get in touch.

## Product Purpose
Marketing site for Qadam School. Success = a parent submits the request form (sent to WhatsApp) or calls the school.

## Brand Personality
Warm, modern, trustworthy. Caring but professional; the orange/yellow brand and real school photos carry the warmth.

## Anti-references
Generic SaaS landing templates; cluttered, noisy school sites; anything that feels cheap or unreliable.

## Design Principles
- The request is the goal: every section should lead toward "leave a request" without friction.
- Everything clickable must do something; nothing that looks like a button may be dead.
- Mobile first: parents read this on phones, so layouts must hold at 360px.
- Consistency builds trust: one content width, aligned edges, steady spacing.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.1 AA basics: readable contrast, labeled form fields, keyboard-usable controls, meaningful alt text, reduced-motion respected for auto-scrolling content.
